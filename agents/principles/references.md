# Principle References

Classic principle sets Patrick reaches for often. They aren't copied here.
Consult the source when a decision calls for it, and record a site-specific
rule in `design.md` or a convention only once it has come up more than once.

| Set | Reach for it when | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dieter Rams, ten principles of good design ("less, but better") | Deciding what to remove, judging whether something is honest, unobtrusive and thorough down to the last detail | [Vitsœ](https://www.vitsoe.com/us/about/good-design) |
| Nielsen Norman Group, 10 usability heuristics | Reviewing an interaction or demo for status visibility, consistency, recognition over recall, and error prevention | [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/) |
| Gestalt principles (proximity, similarity, continuity, closure, figure and ground, common region) | Laying out a slide, card or diagram so grouping and hierarchy read without labels | [IxDF](https://www.interaction-design.org/literature/topics/gestalt-principles) |
| Laws of UX | Checking a choice against a named effect, such as Jakob's Law, Hick's Law, Miller's Law, Tesler's Law or the Doherty Threshold | [lawsofux.com](https://lawsofux.com/) |

Patrick's own heuristics, which several design principles build on:

- **MAYA (most advanced, yet acceptable)**, from Raymond Loewy, and
  **"Default it or design it"**: see
  [10 Heuristics to Simplify Design Decision-Making](https://itspatmorgan.com/writing/10-heuristics-to-simplify-design-decision-making)
  and [Default It or Design It](https://itspatmorgan.com/writing/default-it-or-design-it).
- **Find the single most important thing**, **curation is creation** and
  **published beats perfect**: see
  [50 Principles of Unknown Arts](https://itspatmorgan.com/writing/50-principles-of-unknown-arts).
